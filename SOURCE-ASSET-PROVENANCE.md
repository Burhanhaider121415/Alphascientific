# Source asset provenance

The 22 PNG files in `source-assets/` are byte-identical copies of the numbered originals supplied in the master project folder. Their filenames are preserved. WebP files in `public/assets/` retain the same basenames and are display derivatives created by resizing and re-encoding, leaving the originals unchanged. Reproduction names map directly by extension (`.png` → `.webp`). No source identity or user-selected image 17 composition was replaced.

`public/source/` contains model photographs extracted from the supplied Alfa brochure: COR 50, Abacus 380 and Urilyzer 100 Pro, 500 Pro and Cell. `public/partners/` contains the six original manufacturer marks extracted from that brochure. These assets were compared visually with the brochure. Generated assets 07–09 visibly differ from the brochure product photos and are retained as source material but are not presented as model-exact. Cinematic assets 18–20 are 2D visual material and are not 3D meshes.

The full supplied company brochure is available as `public/documents/alfa-scientific-brochure.pdf`; it is the same file as the source brochure. A link to it must be labelled as a company brochure, not an individual datasheet.
