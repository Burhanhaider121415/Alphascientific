# Source asset provenance

The 22 PNG files in `source-assets/` are byte-identical copies of the numbered originals supplied in the master project folder. Their filenames are preserved. WebP files in `public/assets/` retain the same basenames and are display derivatives created by resizing and re-encoding, leaving the originals unchanged. Reproduction names map directly by extension (`.png` → `.webp`). No source identity or user-selected image 17 composition was replaced.

`public/source/` contains model photographs extracted from the supplied Alfa brochure: COR 50, Abacus 380 and Urilyzer 100 Pro, 500 Pro and Cell. `public/partners/` contains the six original manufacturer marks extracted from that brochure. These assets were compared visually with the brochure. Generated assets 07–09 visibly differ from the brochure product photos and are retained as source material but are not presented as model-exact. Cinematic assets 18–20 are 2D visual material and are not 3D meshes.

The full supplied company brochure is available as `public/documents/alfa-scientific-brochure.pdf`; it is the same file as the source brochure. A link to it must be labelled as a company brochure, not an individual datasheet.

## Stage 06 source-photo framing

The five `public/source/*-framed.png` assets are pixel-identical crops of the already-recovered brochure source PNGs, with a small transparent margin. They remove excess transparent canvas so equipment is legible in responsive cards and product presentations. The original extracted PNGs and the original brochure remain intact. The original and framed files were compared pixel-for-pixel within the recorded crop rectangles on 10 November 2026; no instrument pixels were redrawn or geometries invented.

## Stage 07 creative correction

The COR 50, Abacus 380 and Urilyzer-Cell chapters use the framed brochure photographs, composited in front of browser-rendered Three.js measurement forms. The rings, rails and camera are three-dimensional. The instruments themselves are fixed-view photographs. The available sources lack verified orthogonal side, rear and top views or CAD to reconstruct the full exteriors. ALFA_18–20 remain creative references and are not substituted for exact equipment photography. Product details without verified equipment photographs use a text-led composition. The supplied Animal Chemistry artwork (ALFA_17) remains category-level editorial imagery, without representing individual merchandise. About and Careers retain the approved editorial images; neither identifies the subjects as Alfa employees.

## Final Stage 07 motion amendment

The COR 50 and Abacus 380 retain their verified single-view photographs. Photo layers move with scroll and pointer in separate real-time Three.js scenes. Contact uses the verified Urilyzer 100 Pro photograph with an optical sweep. The Microbial Culture Media art uses a CSS crop and color-field blend from unchanged ALFA_15 artwork. These treatments preserve the underlying files. Category art and manufacturer-linked art are used at the discipline level.

The Urilyzer-Cell inspection includes an original, simplified WebGL exterior study based on the [Analyticon operating manual](https://www.analyticon-diagnostics.com/downloads/user_manual_db/IUCell_en_26_001.pdf), figures 1–2 and the dimensions in section 3.3, and the supplied product photograph. The approved product photograph remains selectable. The study represents the housing, pipetting recess, lower waste bin and controls schematically. It has a limited viewing arc and does not depict the machine interior, provide a complete connector diagram or represent manufacturer CAD.
